Study A

Field notes

Alternating rows with a cycling deck for each hobby. Every image and video has a place in a stack; the text stays still and the photographs keep moving.

04 About

Outside the lab,
I collect hobbies.

Outside academics, I collect 'em like Pokémon. Here are a few of the ones that have stuck.

↻ Six decks, 24 images and clips. Multi-image stacks advance on their own — hover, focus, or use the arrows to hold one.

01

Running

I run 5Ks around the campus two or three times a week. It does wonders for my mental health and my heart, and I enjoy having to push myself through the last bit of a run.

02

Making things by hand

I crochet a lot, and I send postcards to friends with drawings on the back. Until they are posted, they end up being pretty cool decorations in my room.

03

Printmaking

Carving, ink, and the patient art of the reverse image.

04

VR design

Building worlds in virtual space — one of them is called Yggdrasil.

05

Feeding crows

Trying to befriend the local crows. Good progress is being made with mutual recognition.

06

Gaming

Repo, Trailmakers, Space Marine 2, Rain World — and whatever else has a good idea in it.

Contact sheet

Compact filmstrip rows: words on the left, each hobby's photographs in a strip. More hobbies fit into a single pass than in the editorial layout.
